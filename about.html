About the System

Replacing traditional paper notice boards with a clean, instant, web-accessible platform for educational institutions.

Eco-Friendly & Paperless

Eliminates physical printing costs and notice wastage by providing real-time updates directly to student mobile phones and campus computers.

Categorized & Searchable

Students can instantly filter notices by Academic, Examination, Sports, or Event categories, avoiding clutter and finding important announcements fast.

Dedicated Administration

Authorized faculty and department administrators can effortlessly issue emergency alerts, priority tags, and routine schedule changes.
